Gravel Price Index 2026: Planning Ranges

Planning ranges for 11 materials: $20–$50 per short ton and $30–$80 per cubic yard in USD. These are planning estimates, not quotes from named suppliers.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Price Range Table

Every material in this index falls between $20 and $50 per short ton, or $30 and $80 per cubic yard: #57 crushed stone is $25–$40 per ton ($45–$65/yd³) and pea gravel $20–$35 per ton ($30–$50/yd³). These are USD planning ranges, not a supplier survey or an average of named suppliers. Local prices vary with supplier, quantity, haul distance and season, so confirm with at least two local suppliers before ordering.
