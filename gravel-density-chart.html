Notes:

  • Tons per Cubic Yard: Based on a 2,000 lb short ton.
  • Tonnes per Cubic Meter: metric tonnes; 1 tonne = 1,000 kg = 2,205 lb and 1 cubic yard = 0.7646 m³.
  • Range: Typical density range observed across suppliers.
  • Basis: typical published loose bulk density ranges used by this site's calculator. Confirm the figure on your supplier's delivery ticket before ordering, because gradation and moisture move it.
  • Loose vs. compacted: Loose bulk density is shown; compacted density can be 5–15% higher.
  • Moisture: Wet material can weigh 5–10% more; dry material can be 5% lighter.
  • Particle size: Finer materials pack tighter, increasing density.
  • Supplier variation: Always verify with your supplier; values are averages.
