How many bags of gravel do I need?

Enter your project dimensions above to get an instant bag count. The calculator includes waste and compaction allowances and rounds up to ensure you have enough material.

  • Rectangular area: Length (ft) × Width (ft) × Depth (ft) ÷ 27 = cubic yards. Then weight = cubic feet × density (lb/ft³), and bags = weight ÷ bag size, rounded up.
  • Circular area: π × (diameter/2)² × depth ÷ 27 = cubic yards.
  • L‑shaped area: Split into two rectangles, calculate each, then add.
  • Waste allowance (default 10%) adds extra material for settling and spillage.
  • Compaction allowance (default 5%) accounts for material packing down after placement.
  • Results include both full pallets (40 bags each) and any loose bags remaining.

Bags per Short Ton

One short ton is 2,000 lb, so the material density does not change these counts.

  • 50 lb bags: 2,000 ÷ 50 = 40 bags per short ton
  • 40 lb bags: 2,000 ÷ 40 = 50 bags per short ton
  • 60 lb bags: 2,000 ÷ 60 = 33.33, rounded up to 34 bags per short ton

How many bags on a pallet?

Standard configuration: 40 bags per pallet (50 lb bags). Pallets are shrink‑wrapped for transport.

Breakdown:

  • Full pallet: 40 bags (2,000 lb / 1.0 short ton of #57 stone at 50 lb/bag)
  • Typical order: 1 full pallet + N loose bags (e.g., 1 pallet + 12 loose = 52 bags total)
  • Large orders: round up to nearest pallet multiple (e.g., 100 bags = 2 pallets + 20 loose)

Note: Some suppliers use 50 lb bags on pallets; others use 50 kg (≈110 lb) bags. Confirm bag weight with your supplier.

When to Buy Bags vs Bulk

Use this guide to decide based on your project size and budget.

Buy bags when:

  • Project is small, roughly under 0.5 cubic yards (13.5 ft³)
  • You need material quickly (bags are available at most garden centers)
  • You have limited delivery access (no room for a truck)
  • You need precise quantities for small jobs (walkways, small patios)

Buy bulk when:

  • Project is larger than about 0.5 cubic yards
  • You need material for driveways, French drains or large patios
  • The delivered bulk quote is lower per ton than bagged material
  • You have adequate delivery space (truck can drop near the project)

Bag-quantity examples (50 lb bags):

  • 0.5 yd³ #57 stone at 100 lb/ft³: 1,350 lb = 27 bags
  • 1.0 yd³ #57 stone: 2,700 lb = 54 bags
  • At an example $5 per bag, those are $135 and $270 before tax; compare that with the delivered bulk quote.

FAQ

How many bags equal a yard?
It depends on material density and bag weight. For #57 stone at 100 lb/ft³ with 50 lb bags: 2,700 lb ÷ 50 = 54 bags per cubic yard.
How many bags on a pallet?
Standard: 40 bags (50 lb each). Some suppliers use 50 kg (≈110 lb) bags — confirm before ordering.
When is bulk cheaper than bags?
Bulk is usually cheaper per ton once the project is above roughly 0.5 yd³, but delivery fees and minimum order sizes vary. Compare the delivered totals.
Can I mix bag sizes on one pallet?
Most pallets are configured for one bag size. Mixed sizes may require special handling; check with your supplier.
Do I need compaction allowance?
Yes — add 5% for most projects. Add 10–20% for soft subgrade or rounded stone (pea gravel).
