How to convert cubic yards to tons

Formula: tons = cubic yards × density (lb/ft³) ÷ 2000

  • Determine the material's density (lb/ft³). Presets: pea gravel 105, #57 stone 100, river rock 108, masonry sand 110.
  • Multiply cubic yards by density.
  • Divide by 2000 (short tons) or 2204.6 (metric tonnes).
  • Example: 2 yd³ of #57 stone (100 lb/ft³) = 2 × 100 ÷ 2000 = 0.1 short tons.

How much area does 1 ton cover?

Coverage depends on depth. Deeper coverage = less area.

1 ton at 2″ depth covers ~90 ft² (approximately 8.4 m²).

1 ton at 4″ depth covers ~45 ft² (approximately 4.2 m²).

1 ton at 6″ depth covers ~30 ft² (approximately 2.8 m²).

How much will 4 tons cover?

Specific high-volume query example.

  • 4 tons at 2″ depth: ~360 ft² (approx. 33 m²)
  • 4 tons at 4″ depth: ~180 ft² (approx. 17 m²)
  • 4 tons at 6″ depth: ~120 ft² (approx. 11 m²)

Short tons vs Metric Tonnes

The difference is small but relevant for large orders.

  • 1 short ton = 2,000 lb (used in US and most commercial contexts)
  • 1 metric tonne = 2,204.6 lb (used in scientific, international, and some Canadian contexts)
  • Difference: ~10 lb per ton, or about 0.5%
  • For project ordering, the difference is generally negligible unless dealing with very large volumes.

FAQ

How many cubic yards in a ton of gravel?
It depends on density. For #57 stone (100 lb/ft³): ~0.50 cubic yards per ton. For pea gravel (105 lb/ft³): ~0.57 cubic yards per ton.
How much area does 1 ton of gravel cover?
At 2″ depth: ~90 ft². At 4″ depth: ~45 ft². At 6″ depth: ~30 ft². Varies with material density.
What's the difference between short tons and metric tonnes?
Short ton = 2,000 lb (US customary). Metric tonne = 2,204.6 lb. The difference is ~10 lb per ton, or 0.5%.
How much extra gravel should I order?
Add 10% for waste and compaction. For a 2″ deep pathway, order 1.1× the calculated quantity.
Can I convert metric tonnes to cubic yards?
Yes — divide by the material's density (kg/m³) and convert units. The calculator handles this with the metric tonnes toggle.
