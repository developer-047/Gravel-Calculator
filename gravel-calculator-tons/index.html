How to convert cubic yards to tons

Formula: short tons = cubic yards × 27 × density (lb/ft³) ÷ 2,000

  • Determine the material's bulk density (lb/ft³). Presets: pea gravel 105, #57 stone 100, river rock 108, masonry sand 110.
  • Multiply cubic yards by 27 to find cubic feet, then multiply by density to get total weight in pounds.
  • Divide by 2,000 for US short tons, or by 2,204.62 for metric tonnes.
  • Example: 2 yd³ of #57 stone (100 lb/ft³) = 2 × 27 × 100 ÷ 2,000 = 2.70 short tons (about 2.45 metric tonnes).

How much area does 1 ton cover?

Coverage depends on depth and material density. For 1 short ton of pea gravel (105 lb/ft³):

1 ton at 2″ depth covers ~114 ft² (approximately 10.6 m²).

1 ton at 4″ depth covers ~57 ft² (approximately 5.3 m²).

1 ton at 6″ depth covers ~38 ft² (approximately 3.5 m²).

For lighter #57 stone (100 lb/ft³): 1 ton covers ~120 ft² at 2″, ~60 ft² at 4″, and ~40 ft² at 6″.

How much will 4 tons cover?

High-volume project coverage for 4 short tons of pea gravel (105 lb/ft³):

  • 4 tons at 2″ depth: ~457 ft² (approx. 42.5 m²)
  • 4 tons at 4″ depth: ~229 ft² (approx. 21.2 m²)
  • 4 tons at 6″ depth: ~152 ft² (approx. 14.2 m²)

Short tons vs Metric Tonnes

The difference is important when reviewing supplier quotes:

  • 1 short ton = 2,000 lb (standard unit in the US).
  • 1 metric tonne = 2,204.62 lb / 1,000 kg (used internationally and in Canada).
  • Difference: 204.6 lb per ton (a metric tonne is about 10% heavier than a short ton).
  • Always confirm which unit your quarry or supplier uses before placing an order.

FAQ

How many cubic yards in a ton of gravel?
It depends on density. For #57 stone (100 lb/ft³): ~0.74 cubic yards per short ton. For pea gravel (105 lb/ft³): ~0.71 cubic yards per short ton.
How much area does 1 ton of gravel cover?
At 2″ depth: ~114 ft² for pea gravel or ~120 ft² for #57 stone. At 4″ depth: ~57 ft² for pea gravel or ~60 ft² for #57 stone.
What's the difference between short tons and metric tonnes?
Short ton = 2,000 lb. Metric tonne = 2,204.6 lb (1,000 kg). A metric tonne is about 10% heavier (a 205 lb difference).
How much extra gravel should I order?
Add 10% for waste and compaction. For a 2″ deep pathway, order 1.1× the calculated quantity.
Can I convert metric tonnes to cubic yards?
Yes — divide by the material's density (kg/m³) and convert units. The calculator handles this with the metric tonnes toggle.
