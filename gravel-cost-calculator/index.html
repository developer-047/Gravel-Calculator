Waste/Compaction Allowance

Add 0–25% extra material to account for loss during handling, spreading, and compaction.

Typical recommendations:

  • 10% — standard residential use
  • 15% — areas with heavy rainfall or poor drainage
  • 20-25% — large projects, difficult access, or unknown compaction

The waste allowance is applied to both bag and bulk cost calculations.

FAQ

How much does gravel cost per yard?
2026 average: $30–$65 per cubic yard depending on material and region. Pea gravel is typically $30–$50; crushed stone $45–$65; crusher run $35–$55.
How much does gravel cost per ton?
2026 average: $15–$35 per short ton. Pea gravel $15–$30; #57 stone $25–$35; crusher run $20–$30.
Are bags or bulk cheaper?
Bulk is almost always cheaper per unit for quantities over 0.5 cubic yards. Bags are convenient for small projects (< 0.5 yd³) or limited access. Typical savings: 20–30% with bulk.
How much extra gravel should I order?
Add 10% for waste and compaction as a default. Add 15-25% for difficult access, steep slopes, or if this is the first time spreading this material.
Can I mix bag sizes?
Yes, but the calculator assumes one bag size per calculation. Mix sizes manually or run separate calculations for each.
