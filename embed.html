Embed code

Copy this block into your page HTML. Keep the iframe and the attribution paragraph together.

<iframe src="https://www.progravelcalculator.online/gravel-calculator-widget.html"
        width="100%" height="520" style="border:0;max-width:100%"
        title="Gravel calculator widget" loading="lazy"></iframe>
<p><a href="https://www.progravelcalculator.online/">Gravel Calculator</a>
   by Pro Gravel Calculator</p>

The widget is a fixed-height iframe, so the height stays readable on phones and desktops. Use 560 or more if your template adds padding around embeds. Width is fluid, so it fits any column.

Attribution

The attribution line is part of the embed and must stay visible directly under the widget:

  • Keep the link to progravelcalculator.online in the paragraph under the iframe.
  • Do not hide the attribution with CSS, a collapsed container or a scroll box.
  • Do not remove, rewrite or self-host widget.js or the calculator page behind the iframe; both are updated together.
  • Embedding is free. There is nothing to sign up for and no ad code is injected.

What the widget calculates

The widget runs the same calculation engine as the full calculator, so the numbers match this site exactly at the widget's settings. It covers:

  • Rectangle, circle and L-shaped areas, in feet and inches or metres and centimetres.
  • Pea gravel (105 lb/ft³), crushed stone (100 lb/ft³), river rock (108 lb/ft³) and sand (110 lb/ft³) at typical loose bulk density.
  • Outputs in cubic yards or cubic metres, short tons or metric tonnes, and 50 lb or 25 kg bags.

Waste allowance, compaction, cost estimates and the printable order sheet stay on the full calculator, which the attribution link opens.

Privacy and analytics

The widget page loads the calculator engine and its controller only. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics, so embedding it does not add a consent banner or a third-party request to your page.
