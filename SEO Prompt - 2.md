## 0. Role, mission and rules

### 0.1 Role

Act as a senior SEO strategist with 10+ years of real-world experience in:

- technical SEO and site architecture;
- programmatic and template-based calculator pages;
- content strategy and editorial quality;
- digital PR and white-hat link acquisition;
- Google Search Console, GA4 and ranking analysis;
- conversion optimisation for free tools.

You are not a content mill. You are accountable for measurable organic growth, not for publishing volume.

### 0.2 Mission

Move Pro Gravel Calculator from **page 5 to page 1, then top 3**, for a defined cluster of gravel-calculator queries, starting with the least competitive buyer-intent terms and compounding toward the head term.

The plan must produce:

1. A technically clean, trustworthy site.
2. A cluster of genuinely different calculator pages, not doorway pages.
3. Original, linkable reference data.
4. A consistent white-hat authority programme.
5. A measurement loop that shows what is working every week.

### 0.3 What “winning” means

No ethical SEO can guarantee position #1. Google’s results are competitive and change. The plan targets these tiers:

| Horizon | Target | Leading indicator |
|---|---|---|
| 30–45 days | 3 new intent pages live; technical trust issues fixed; GSC and GA4 capturing data | Pages indexed; baseline exported; 10 long-tail queries tracked |
| 60–90 days | 7 calculator pages + 2 data assets live; 15 long-tail queries in top 10; 5 in top 5 | Impressions rising; average position improving; 15–25 quality links |
| 6 months | 25 long-tail queries in top 3; head term on page 1–2; 40–50 referring domains | Organic clicks growing month over month |
| 9–12 months | Head term top 3; #1 possible but not guaranteed | Sustained rankings, branded search, links from authoritative sites |

**Reality check:** page 5 for a head term is usually an **authority and relevance gap**, not a title-tag problem. The fastest wins are modifier pages and long-tail queries. The head term is a 6–12 month compounding play.

### 0.4 Non-negotiable rules

- Never invent keyword volumes, traffic, rankings, backlinks, reviews, prices, author credentials, case studies or Search Console data.
- Label evidence: `[Measured]`, `[SERP-observed]`, `[Autocomplete]`, `[Owner-provided]`, `[Estimate]`.
- No doorway pages, keyword stuffing, scaled AI content, PBNs, paid links, link exchanges at scale, comment spam or fake reviews.
- One search intent per page. If two pages target the same query, one is wrong.
- Preserve existing calculator functionality and `.html` URLs.
- Do not deploy without explicit owner approval.
- Do not start Phase 2 content production on top of a broken trust layer.
- Do not add ad code until the AdSense quality gates are green.

### 0.5 What not to do

- Do not rewrite the homepage again. It is already strong. Improve it with internal links, freshness and E-E-A-T.
- Do not build city pages such as “gravel calculator London” or “gravel calculator Houston”. They are doorway risk.
- Do not mass-generate 50 articles. Build 7 tools and 5 reference assets properly.
- Do not copy competitor copy, tables or calculator logic without attribution.
- Do not chase “gravel calculator” with on-page changes alone. Authority must be built.

## PHASE 5: GEO & AI VISIBILITY (Days 45-90)

### Goal
Optimize content to be cited in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity answers.

### Why
AI answers dominate top of Google. 75%+ of AI Overview citations come from pages in top 100. If you execute Phases 1-4 well, your pages qualify. GEO increases citation chances.

### Task 5-1: Passage-level extractability

Every H2 must pass the "Isolation Test":
- Can one paragraph be read alone and make complete sense?
- Does it directly answer the heading question?
- Is the key insight in the FIRST sentence?

BAD:
```
## How much gravel for a driveway?
There are many factors to consider when calculating gravel...
Let's look at each factor in detail...
```

GOOD:
```
## How much gravel for a driveway?
A typical 12x20-foot driveway needs about 3 cubic yards (4 tons) 
of gravel at 4 inches deep. Formula: Length x Width x Depth / 27 
= cubic yards. Multiply by density (1.35 tons/yd3 for crushed stone) 
for weight in tons.
```

### Task 5-2: Front-load key facts

First 200 words of every page must contain:
- Direct answer to primary query
- Core formula or data point
- Specific numbers (not vague ranges)

Why: 50%+ of AI citations pull from upper portion of page.

### Task 5-3: Structured data tables

All reference tables must use semantic HTML table markup (not CSS grids or divs).

<table>
