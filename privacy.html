Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what Pro Gravel Calculator collects, why, and the choices you have. It covers the calculator, the guides and the contact page at progravelcalculator.online.

The short version

  • Calculator inputs stay in your browser. We do not receive your measurements, material choice or results.
  • We do not run third-party advertising today. If that changes, this policy will be updated before ads go live.
  • Analytics is optional and loads only after you press Accept analytics.
  • If you send a quote request or correction, we use your details only to respond.

Calculator inputs

All calculations happen locally in your browser using JavaScript. Dimensions, shape, depth and material selection are not transmitted to our servers, stored in a database or linked to your identity. If you use the share button, the recipient receives a link; check the link before sharing it.

Information you choose to provide

The contact page includes a quote-request form. When form delivery is enabled, the information you enter — such as your name, email address, phone number and project details — is used solely to review and respond to your request. We do not sell it or add you to a marketing list.

Please do not send sensitive personal information, payment details or site plans you are not authorised to share. Quote requests are not a secure channel for financial data.

Analytics and consent

Analytics is optional and off until you accept. We use Google Analytics 4. Until you choose Accept analytics, no Google tag is loaded, no analytics cookies are set and no usage data is collected. Declining analytics has no effect on the calculator.

When you accept, the Google Analytics 4 tag loads so we can understand which pages and calculator features are useful. You can change your choice at any time using the control. Turning analytics off stops the tag from receiving further events on that page.

Once enabled, Google Analytics may collect page views, approximate location (derived from IP address), device and browser information, referral source, and interactions such as calculator completion, print, share and outbound quote clicks. We do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers or the dimensions you enter to analytics. Google states that Google Analytics 4 does not log or store individual IP addresses; see Google's Analytics data collection explanation.

The consent record is kept for up to 12 months so we can respect your choice. Analytics cookies are retained according to the Google Analytics settings, typically up to 14 months. Declining analytics has no effect on the calculator.

Advertising

We do not currently display third-party ads, and no advertising script is installed. If we add Google AdSense or another advertising partner, we will update this policy, add the required consent controls, and give you a way to manage personalised advertising before any ads are served. The presence of an ads.txt file does not mean ads are live.

Service providers

  • Vercel hosts the site and processes standard server logs such as IP address, request time and user agent for security and reliability.
  • Google Analytics processes analytics data only after consent, under Google's terms and privacy policy.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) delivers the optional PDF library when you press Save PDF. That request is made only when you use the feature.

These providers may process data in the United States or other countries. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Legal bases

For visitors in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, we rely on: consent for analytics and any future advertising cookies; legitimate interests for site security, debugging and responding to messages; and, where applicable, performance of a request you have made. You may withdraw consent at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, object to or restrict processing, or withdraw consent. California residents may have rights to know, delete, correct and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

To make a request, use the contact page and describe the data and right involved. We may need to verify your identity before acting. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Retention and security

Quote-request messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of the enquiry, then deleted or anonymised. The site is served over HTTPS. Because the calculator is static and browser-based, there is no database of calculator inputs to breach. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Children

The site is intended for homeowners, contractors and other adults planning material purchases. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will update the date at the top when this policy changes. Material changes — such as enabling advertising or a new analytics provider — will be announced on this page and, where required, through a new consent request.

Contact

Questions about this policy or a data request can be sent through the contact page. Please include enough detail for us to identify the page or message concerned. We do not publish a postal address.
