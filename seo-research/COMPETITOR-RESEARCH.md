# Competitor Research — Gravel Calculator Niche

> **Research date:** 2026-11-01
> **Method:** Live HTTP fetches (curl, desktop user agent, en-US), DNS lookups, Google web search, and Google Autocomplete. No paid SEO tool was used.
> **Rule applied:** no traffic, keyword-volume, domain-authority, ranking or backlink numbers are invented. Where a metric could not be obtained, this report says so.
> **Evidence labels:** `[Measured]` = directly observed in HTTP response or code. `[SERP-observed]` = title/snippet seen in a live Google result. `[Estimate]` = analyst judgment. `[Unavailable]` = not measurable with the tools used.

---

## 1. Summary

The niche is crowded at the top by **general-purpose calculator authorities** (Omni Calculator, Inch Calculator, CalculatorSoup, GigaCalculator, Calculator.net) and increasingly by **single-purpose gravel sites** that have built 10–40 page calculator clusters (`gravelcalculation.com`, `gravel-calc.com`, `calculategravel.com`, `peagravelcalculator.com`, `gravelcalculate.com`).

Two of the three competitors named in the brief — **`aggregatecalc.com` and `materialpal.com` — did not resolve in DNS on 2026-11-01** and could not be analysed. A third, **`thegravelcalculator.com`, is live** and is a direct product-level competitor: same "free, no signup" positioning, three material categories, and a photo/auto-detect measurement feature.

The practical conclusion: Pro Gravel Calculator cannot win by being "another gravel calculator". It can win by being the **most trustworthy and most complete order-planning tool** — one that handles layers, waste, compaction, bag-vs-bulk, delivery and shareable/printable estimates — and by owning **specific intents** rather than the generic head term.

## 4. Detailed competitor profiles

### 4.1 The Gravel Calculator — `thegravelcalculator.com`

**Direct product competitor.** `[Measured]`

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Positioning | "Free — no signup required" |
| Structure | Homepage hub + `/gravel`, `/soil`, `/mulch` |
| Gravel page title | `Gravel Calculator - Free Online Gravel Calculator for Driveways, Walkways & Patios` (**86 characters**) |
| Gravel page description | 193 characters — likely truncated in SERPs |
| Canonical | Self-referencing to `/gravel` |
| Materials | "10+ gravel types", 6 soil types, 6 mulch types |
| Measurement modes | Auto-detect common shapes, manual entry, **photo scale** |
| Outputs | Volume, weight, cost; compaction adjustment mentioned |
| Trust content | About, Privacy, Terms, Contact |
| Design | Minimal, dark/light theme toggle, card-based hub |

**What they do well**
- Clear three-material hub (gravel / soil / mulch) that captures adjacent landscaping queries.
- "Photo scale" is a genuine differentiator and a strong click driver.
- Simple, fast, mobile-first presentation.
- Metadata targets "driveways, walkways & patios" rather than only "gravel calculator".

**Weaknesses / openings**
- Title and description exceed SERP limits, so the differentiator ("photo scale", "10+ types") may be cut.
- The cluster is shallow: no dedicated driveway, pea gravel, crushed stone, cost, bag or French drain pages were observed.
- Photo measurement is impressive but can be inaccurate; no visible methodology or density source was observed on the homepage.
- No visible project-layer logic (base + surface), no bag-size choice, no delivery estimate.

**What Pro Gravel Calculator should do differently**
- Beat them on **order accuracy**: layers, compaction, waste, bag-vs-bulk, delivery.
- Beat them on **transparency**: visible density table with sources and a methodology note.
- Do **not** copy the photo feature in the first 90 days; it is expensive and does not create defensible SEO value.

## 5. Patterns across the strongest competitors

1. **Every serious competitor has a calculator cluster, not one page.** Typically 6–20 pages covering driveway, pea gravel, crushed stone, cost, bags/coverage, French drain, and conversions.
2. **Cost is table stakes.** Omni, Inch, CalculatorSoup, CalculateGravel, GravelCalculation, PeaGravelCalculator and the UK sites all include a price input. Pro Gravel Calculator does not.
3. **Compaction and waste are expected.** CalculatorSoup separates them; CalculateGravel uses a buffer slider; GroundCalc uses +12%; RemodelCalculators bakes in a compaction multiplier.
4. **Bag counts are inconsistently served.** Only a few competitors do bag math well, and almost none support **multiple bag sizes**. This is a clear opening.
5. **Trust is the weakest area across the niche.** Most sites publish density and cost numbers with no source, no author, no review date. The exceptions (Omni, Inch, CalcSummit, PeaGravelCalculator) visibly invest in methodology.
6. **Titles are usually short and exact.** Omni's 48-character title and CalculatorSoup's 17-character title contrast sharply with The Gravel Calculator's 86 and Pro Gravel Calculator's 80.
7. **Few competitors offer a shareable or printable estimate.** Print/PDF is a genuine differentiator already present on Pro Gravel Calculator — but the share button does not currently encode the calculation.
8. **Schema coverage is uneven.** Omni, Inch, GigaCalculator and the UK aggregate site use Article/FAQ/Person/SoftwareApplication. CalculatorSoup had none in the fetched HTML. Pro Gravel Calculator has schema but with a duplicate FAQ conflict.

## 7. What this means for Pro Gravel Calculator

**Do not compete on:**
- Number of materials alone. CalculatorSoup and CalculateGravel already have 12–14 and custom density.
- Photo measurement. The Gravel Calculator already owns that message; it is costly and accuracy-risky.
- Generic calculator breadth. Omni and Inch will out-authority a small site on "calculator" head terms.

**Compete on:**
1. **Order accuracy** — separate compaction and waste, base + surface layers, multiple bag sizes, bulk-vs-bag comparison, delivery cost.
2. **Trust** — visible density table with sources, methodology page, named reviewer, last-reviewed date, transparent assumptions. Most of the niche is weak here.
3. **Output usefulness** — a clean printable/shareable "supplier-ready" estimate with material, volume, weight, bags, buffer and cost.
4. **Intent-specific tools** — 7 focused calculators that are genuinely pre-configured, not doorway copies.
5. **US-first localisation** — cubic yards, short tons, #57/#411, 50 lb bags, USD, while keeping a clean metric toggle.

**Positioning sentence to build around:**
> "Pro Gravel Calculator gives you a supplier-ready gravel estimate — volume, weight, bags, waste, compaction and cost — for the exact project you are building."
